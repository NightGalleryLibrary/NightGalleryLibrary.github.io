Myths & Monsters

Forty windows. Numbered, then filled.

Medusa

with somewhere to be

Hecate

three faces at the joining

Persephone

six seeds, and the year divides

Circe

the bronze bowl, and the year they stayed

Minotaur

the heart of the maze

Cerberus

three throats at the gate

Hydra

cut one, two more

Sirens

the cliff, and the wreck

Chimera

lion, goat, and the fire

Sphinx

the riddle under her paws

Cyclops

one eye, a whole flock

Nemean Lion

hide that will not take a blade

Fenrir

a god's hand still in his mouth

Jörmungandr

the sea that bites its tail

Hel

half living, half already gone

Yggdrasil

nine worlds in the wood

Valkyrie

she names who rides north

Odin

the empty socket, the two ravens

Huginn

thought, and the other circling

Sleipnir

eight legs across the sky

Baba Yaga

the house stands on chicken legs

Cernunnos

antlers, torc, and the grove

Firebird

the apple tree at midnight

Leshy

the wood that walks

Phoenix

five hundred years, then fire

Griffin

one claw on the hoard

Dragon

the cairn, and the broken lance

Basilisk

the glance that empties a cloister

Manticore

a human mouth on a lion

Unicorn

the glade, and the spiral

Kitsune

nine tails in the courtyard

Dryad

bark becoming skin

Satyr

goat legs in the vines

Pegasus

the spring under the hooves

Centaur

the bow drawn at a gallop

Harpy

the storm, and the wrecked table

Kraken

the mast in the tentacle

Leviathan

coils like hills

Banshee

the keep, and the comb

Anubis

the heart, and the feather

Forty windows. The rest live in the book.
